# MyUGC 30-Day Product-Led Content Plan (EN-first)

## Publishing Rule
- 1 article/day
- max 5 articles/week
- off days: Thursday, Sunday
- publish days: Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Friday, Saturday

## Mandatory Article Standard
- one cluster, one primary intent
- 1200–1800 words
- practical guide structure (not FAQ)
- required blocks: workflow, checklist, use case, common mistakes
- final internal links: same cluster only

## Forbidden
- best/top/hack/guru roundups
- broad comparison pages without strong intent

## Post-publish for each article
- +2 links to old core posts in same cluster
- +2 inbound links from old core posts to new article
- add in cluster block (same cluster only)
- canonical QA
- hreflang QA (only when translated)
- include in sitemap only after QA

## Translation mode
- publish EN first
- after indexation/quality: translate strongest 5–10 only
